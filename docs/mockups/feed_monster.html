Little Arcade · counting game

Feed the Monster

He shows how many snacks he wants. Tap the bowl once for each bite and watch his tummy fill. You can also tap a matching group of snacks to feed that many at once.

Try a round

Live game

How a round works

  1. 1Look at the snack bubble. It shows how many bites the monster wants.
  2. 2Feed that many. Tap the bowl once per bite, or tap the matching snack group below.
  3. 3Watch the tummy. Each filled dot is one bite. When they match, the monster celebrates.

No score and no wrong answer. The game uses pictures and sounds, and the same round is playable by touch with voice off.
