Rainbow Piano: 320×240 landscape mockup

Five big, differently shaped rainbow keys play in free play. Three picture buttons change the sound. The gentle follow-light is optional: any key always plays, and nothing is lost. Tap Home to return to the launcher; tap the piano tile to play again.

Keyboard: 1–5 = keys, B/F/D = bells/flute/drum, A = AGAIN, Home = launcher
