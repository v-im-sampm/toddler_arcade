Beat Sharks: 320×240 landscape mockup (with working audio)

Tap the water to add a shark, and tap a shark to remove it. The fin swims along the bottom and every shark it passes sings: big blue shark = BOOM, orange shark = tap, small purple shark = your sound. The red button records a new sound for the purple row (up to 1.5 s; the loop pauses and the speaker is muted while recording). Tempo cycles turtle → shark → rabbit. Fill every column for a “full ocean” wave.


Keyboard: Q–Y / A–H / Z–N = cells (top/middle/bottom row), Space = play/stop, Enter = record, . = tempo, Esc = Home
