Main menu + Hatch-a-Pet: 320×240 landscape mockup

Menu: big picture tiles in the SceneManager tile rects (4 per page), with no text to read. The next-page arrow appears only when there are more than 4 games. Home is hidden on the menu (you are already home); Mic stays. Hatch-a-Pet: tap the egg 3–5 times to crack it, and a surprise creature hatches (8 original creatures). Then you pet it (heart / tap the creature) or ask for another egg (egg button). Every tap makes progress. There is no fail state and no text.

Voice keys: C = "colors", E = "eggs", Backspace = BACK, Y = YES, N = NO, A = AGAIN, H = HELP
