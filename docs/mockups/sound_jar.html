Sound Jar: 320×240 landscape mockup (with working audio)

Tap the red button and make a silly sound (up to 3 s; the speaker is muted while recording). Then tap the five creatures — big creature = low voice, tiny creature = high voice. Hold a creature to loop; while holding, slide sideways to bend the pitch. Toggle Backwards, Echo, Robot and Wobble; they stack. Tap the jar to hear the sound plainly. Before any recording, the jar holds a built-in “boing”.


Keyboard: R = record/stop, 1–5 = creatures (hold = loop), Space = jar, B/E/O/W = effects, A = AGAIN, Esc = Home
